About our home

A Pigeon Forge vacation home with vintage national park vibes. Settle in with fast Wi-Fi, plush beds, a full kitchen, and a workstation before heading out to explore the Smokies.

Nestled on a calm dead-end street in Pigeon Forge, The Rambler’s Rest blends rustic textures with clean, modern finishes. Dollywood and The Island are nearby, while Patriot Park, its mass transit station, and the Great Smoky Mountains Parkway are a short walk away. Close to the fun, yet tucked away.

Start your day with mountain views, stroll to local shops, and unwind with a movie after a day of exploring. We’ve made this a comfortable place to return to between Pigeon Forge and Smoky Mountain adventures.
